Chapel Street, Ashwell

One bookat a time

A Baptist church that has been working its way through the Bible since 1841 and is currently about a third of the way into Romans. Two services on a Sunday, and coffee that is better than it used to be.

Coming for the first time

Sundays

Two services, and a prayer meeting in the week.

Nothing on a Sunday requires you to have been before, to stand up, or to say anything.

What we are preaching

A book at a time, in order, however long it takes.
