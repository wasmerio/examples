Mira Okafor is made up. So are her projects, her papers and her articles. They are here so that you can see every part of the mksite theme with content that looks like the real thing.

## What to try

Open any paper under [Research](/research/). The PDF opens in a reader on the page. You can select text, zoom, jump to a page or download the file. Above it there is a **Cite this paper** box with BibTeX and a plain citation, both made from the paper's front matter.

Press <kbd>/</kbd> on any page to search. Open [All work](/work/) to see everything in one dated list, and use the buttons at the top to show only one kind. That filter works with JavaScript turned off.

Scroll down an article and watch the thin line at the top of the window. Print any page and the menus disappear. The squares in the footer change the colour.

The [résumé](/resume/) is built from the same files as the rest of the site. You can save it as a PDF from the print window or download the ready-made file.

## Behind the scenes

Each paper is a folder with an `index.md` and a `paper.pdf`. The theme finds the PDF, adds the tags that Google Scholar reads, and shows the reader. A project can list related papers or articles in its front matter, and the pages it points to link back to it.
