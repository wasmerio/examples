This is a sample post written to show off the theme. Swap it for your own words, photos and stories. Short paragraphs, clear headings and a good image go a long way.

## Start with a story

Readers love to know the why behind the things you make. Tell them where an idea came from, what went wrong and what you would do differently next time.

> Make fewer things, and make them well.

A few things that help every post:

- One clear idea per post
- A strong opening line
- An image that sets the mood

Finish with a simple next step, like a link to your newsletter or your latest project.
