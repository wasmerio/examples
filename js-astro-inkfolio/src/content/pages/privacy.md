This site is a static website. There is no account, no login, no form that stores your data, and no newsletter list — so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to delete later.

## What is collected

If analytics are switched on, the site records page views: which page, roughly where in the world, which browser, and whether the visit came from a search result or a link. If you declined the cookie prompt, anything that sets cookies stays switched off.

Server logs at the hosting provider record IP addresses for a short period for security and abuse prevention, as every web host does.

## What is not collected

- No names, no email addresses, no accounts
- No advertising identifiers, no cross-site tracking
- No data sold or shared with third parties beyond the analytics provider named in the cookie prompt

## Cookies

Cookies are only set by the analytics provider, and only after you accept. Your choice is stored locally in your browser so the site does not ask again. Clear your browser storage to be asked again.
