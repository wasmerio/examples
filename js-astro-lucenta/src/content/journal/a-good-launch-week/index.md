Launches fail quietly. Not with a crash, but with a typo on the pricing page that nobody notices for a month. So we treat launch week as part of the project, not the end of it.

## Before

Two days before, we freeze changes and walk every page on every device the client's customers use. We check the emails, the receipts and the 404 page. We test the slowest phone in the studio on hotel wifi.

## During

Someone from the studio is in the room on launch day. We watch real people use the site, keep a shared list of everything odd, and fix the small things the same afternoon.

## After

We stay on for ninety days. The best ideas for version two always come from the first week of real customers, and we would rather hear them than read about them.
