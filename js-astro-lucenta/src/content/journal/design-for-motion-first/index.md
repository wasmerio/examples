A logo used to live on a letterhead. Now it lives in a six-second story, a loading state and a product page that scrolls. So we ask a new first question: how does this brand move?

## Behaviour before form

For Lumio we wrote three verbs before we drew a single frame: glow, warm, settle. Every transition on the site does one of those. Nothing bounces, nothing spins, and the whole brand feels calmer for it.

## Rules, not animations

We hand over motion as rules rather than files. Durations, easing curves and what is allowed to move together. A team can make a hundred new pieces from five rules. They cannot from one beautiful animation.

## Start small

Try it on your next project: before the logo review, show the logo arriving on screen. You will learn more in five seconds than in a whole deck of static options.
