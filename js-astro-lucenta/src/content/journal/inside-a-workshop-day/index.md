Every project starts with a day together. People ask what happens in it, so here is a typical one.

## Morning: listen

We start with the founders' story, told badly on purpose: no slides, just talking. Then customers, on the phone or in the room. Then the competition, printed out and pinned to the wall.

## Afternoon: sort

By lunch the wall is full. In the afternoon we sort it into three piles: what to keep, what to lose and what nobody has noticed yet. The third pile is where most identities come from.

## Evening: one sentence

We do not leave until we have one sentence everyone agrees on. It is never the tagline, and it is rarely elegant. But every decision for the next three months gets checked against it.
