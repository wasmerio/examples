We launched the Kiln shop last summer with one strong idea: shoot every piece on the same butter yellow, in the same hard sun. A year later, we sat down with the studio to see how it had held up.

## What worked

The shoot system. Kiln's team now photographs every new piece themselves in under ten minutes, and the shop still looks like one long still life. Repeat customers are at forty-one percent, and the "gone" state for sold pieces turned out to be the most visited page on the site.

## What broke

Shipping. One-off pieces mean one-off box sizes, and our checkout assumed three. We rebuilt the shipping rules in the spring.

## What they changed without us

They added a second yellow for the holiday collection. It is slightly warmer. It is better than ours.
