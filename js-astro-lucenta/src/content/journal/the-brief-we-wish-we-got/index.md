We read a lot of briefs. The best ones are short, and they answer a handful of questions honestly.

## Six questions

1. **What is changing?** A new product, a new market, a new competitor. Something made you write this.
2. **Who has to love it?** Not "millennials". A person, with a job and a reason to care.
3. **What do people already say about you?** The good and the bad, in their words.
4. **What will you never do?** Constraints are more useful than ambitions.
5. **How will you know it worked?** A number, a feeling, a phone call you want to get.
6. **What is the budget?** We would rather design a great small thing than a timid big one.

## What matters less

Mood boards of other brands, long lists of adjectives, and the deliverables list. We will write that together after the first workshop, once we know what the real problem is.
