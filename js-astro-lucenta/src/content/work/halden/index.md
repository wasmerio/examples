## The brief

Halden had outgrown a logo drawn by a friend and a bag that looked like every other specialty roaster on the shelf. They were about to open three cafés and start selling to supermarkets, and they needed a brand that could hold its own at both.

## The idea

In the first workshop someone mentioned, almost in passing, that Halden only roasts before noon, "when the light is right". Customers had been repeating it for years. We made it the whole story: a family of twelve suns, one for every coffee, each drawn to match how the roast tastes, from a soft morning glow to a hard midday blaze.

## What we made

A cobalt and terracotta palette borrowed from Lisbon's tiles, a warm grotesque for the wordmark, and a modular bag system that turns a shelf of twelve coffees into a sunrise. The same suns run through the café signage, the cups and the website, so you know where you are before you read a word.
