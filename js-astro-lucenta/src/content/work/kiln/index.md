## The brief

Kiln sold out every market stall it had. Online it was a template shop with phone photos on grey tables. Every piece is one of a kind, and none of that came through.

## The idea

One colour, one light. Every piece is photographed on the same butter yellow in the same hard afternoon sun, so the glazes do all the talking and the whole shop reads like one long still life.

## What we made

Art direction and a shoot system the studio can repeat themselves, a storefront built around one-off pieces with a "gone" state that keeps sold work on display, and a launch campaign that ran on posters across Brooklyn.
