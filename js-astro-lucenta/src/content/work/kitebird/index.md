## The brief

Two audio engineers had built a great-sounding pair of headphones at a fair price. In a category of black, silver and serious, they needed a brand that would get noticed in a feed before anyone heard a note.

## The idea

Take sound seriously and nothing else. Every image pairs the product with one absurd, perfectly lit prop on a candy-coloured backdrop, and every line of copy is a joke with a real spec in it.

## What we made

The name and a chunky rounded wordmark, a six-colour range with packaging that opens like a gift, a still-life photography system the team now shoots in-house, and a launch site that took £1.4M in pre-orders.
