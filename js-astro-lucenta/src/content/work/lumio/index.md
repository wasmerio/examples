## The brief

Lumio makes beautiful lamps and was selling them on a white website, photographed switched off. Their products looked like furniture. Sales happened in showrooms, not online, and the team wanted that to change before they launched in the US.

## The idea

A lamp is only finished when it is on. We built the whole experience in the dark: black pages, products lit from inside, and a scroll that slowly warms the room as you read. Every product page opens on the lamp switched on and lets you dim it with your cursor.

## What we made

A new art direction for all product photography, a refreshed wordmark with a thinner, glowing stroke, and a fast storefront with a custom product viewer. The site launched in English and Danish with a shop that the in-house team runs without us.
