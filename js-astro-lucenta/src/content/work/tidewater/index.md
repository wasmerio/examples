## The brief

A family turning a 1970s pink apartment block on the coast into a small hotel. They wanted guests to feel the building, not a brand stuck on top of it.

## The idea

We matched the identity to the plaster. Every colour in the system was sampled from the walls, tiles and pool at different times of day, so the printed matter looks like it was made on site.

## What we made

A stepped wordmark drawn from the stairways, signage cast in the same pink plaster as the walls, room keys, menus and an opening campaign shot on the property over one long weekend.
