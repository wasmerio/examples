## The brief

Veldt makes technical clothing for people who walk a long way. Their campaigns looked like every outdoor brand: close-ups of zips, rugged faces, mountains at sunset.

## The idea

Scale. We put the person tiny in an enormous landscape, always in Veldt orange, so every image reads as a single dot of colour in a huge, calm field. It works on a billboard and on a phone at the same time.

## What we made

Three shoots in three deserts, a set of stills and short films cut for every format, a campaign microsite and the art direction rules Veldt now uses for every season.
