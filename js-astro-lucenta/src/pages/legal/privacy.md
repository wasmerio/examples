## Who we are

Lucenta Studio Ltd ("Lucenta", "we") runs this website and is the controller of the personal information described here. You can reach us about privacy at privacy@example.com.

## What we collect

- **Project enquiries.** When you send an enquiry, we keep your name, email, company, and what you tell us about the project, including any link you share.
- **Messages.** When you use the contact form or write to one of our inboxes, we keep your message and the details you include with it.
- **Usage.** Our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

## How we use it

We use enquiries and messages to reply to you and to prepare estimates. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to two years, so we can pick up a conversation later, unless you ask us to delete yours sooner. Messages are kept for as long as the conversation needs.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can object to how we use it. Write to privacy@example.com and we will reply within a month.

## Cookies

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, this page will say so and we will ask for your consent first.

## Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the date of the latest version at the top.
