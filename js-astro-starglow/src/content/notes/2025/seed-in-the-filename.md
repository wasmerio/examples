---
title: Put the seed in the filename
description: The cheapest way to find a good generative output again.
tags: [generative-art]
updated: 2025-12-04
---

Every export from my sketches is named `sketchname-seed-width.png`, for example `drift-48213-2400.png`. When a print turns out well months later, the seed is right there and I can render it again at any size.
