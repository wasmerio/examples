I am Mara Lindqvist, a writer and editor. For twelve years I have edited other people's books and essays, mostly nonfiction, and in the gaps I write my own. This site is where the shorter things go: notes on the craft, on tools, on reading, and on paying attention when the day makes that difficult.

Nothing here is finished in the way a book is finished. Posts get corrected when I find a mistake and occasionally rewritten when I change my mind. The date at the top of each one tells you which version of me wrote it.

## Working with me

I take on a small number of editing projects each year, usually book-length nonfiction and long essays. If you have a manuscript and a deadline that is not next week, write to me at [mara@example.com](mailto:mara@example.com).

## About this site

The site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and a theme called [Plain](https://tessera.hevislav.workers.dev/themes/plain), set in Newsreader. There is no analytics script and no comment box. If you want to respond to something, the address above works, and so does the [RSS feed](/rss.xml).
