Sending film to a lab costs about $15 a roll with scans. I shoot a lot of black and white, so a home setup paid for itself in a season.

## What it cost

- A used developing tank and two reels: $35.
- A changing bag: $25.
- Chemicals for about forty rolls: $70.
- A blackout curtain and tape: $30.
- A clip-on fan: $20.

That is $180, and the closet still holds coats.

## Start here

If I started again, I would buy the changing bag first. With it, the only step that needs real darkness happens inside a bag on the kitchen table.
