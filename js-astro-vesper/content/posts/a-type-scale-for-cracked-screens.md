We counted: eleven of the twenty phones on our last field test had a cracked screen. The cracks are not going to be fixed, so the type has to work through them.

## Fewer sizes, heavier weights

The map uses four sizes and two weights. The smallest label is 13 pixels in semibold; anything smaller vanished behind the cracks in our tests.

## Room around every label

Each label gets a halo the color of the map behind it, so a line of the trail never runs through a word. It costs a little drawing time and saves a lot of squinting.
