I have walked the fence trail on Mount Tamalpais six times. On the seventh, the fog came in below the ridge and the fence ran straight into a white wall.

## Walk it again

The best photographs I have made came from places I already knew. A new trail asks for attention just to stay on it. A familiar one leaves room to look.

One roll, thirty-six frames, and the fence in nearly all of them.
