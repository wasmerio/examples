Seven days, sixty-two miles and a lot of switchbacks. Here is everything that went in the camera half of the pack, with its weight.

## The list

- Two 35mm bodies, one loaded with color and one with black and white: 1.4 lb each.
- A 28mm, a 50mm and a 90mm: 1.6 lb together.
- Eleven rolls of film in a zip bag: 0.6 lb.
- A pocket notebook and a pencil: 2 oz.

## Next time

The 90mm came home with four frames on it. It stays home. The thing I wished I had packed was a second lens cloth, after the first one went into a creek on day two.
