Every project at Switchback Studio gets its own pocket notebook. The pages are numbered, the date goes at the top, and nothing ever gets torn out.

## Why paper

A notebook has no tabs and no notifications. When I sketch a screen on paper, I sketch the one screen, not the whole app around it.

## Into the repository

At the end of each week I photograph the new pages and add them to the project's repository, in a folder called notes. Two years later, it is the fastest way to answer "why did we do it this way?"
