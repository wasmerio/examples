The brief for Ridgeline was one line long: a trail report tool that works without a signal. We had a dozen ideas for it, and the crew we joined in Georgia threw out most of them by lunch on the first day.

## What we learned

Crews write reports at the end of the day, sitting on a log, not on the trail. They want a list of what is left to do, not a feed of what was done. And nobody looks at a phone while holding a saw.

## What changed

The report form moved from the map to its own screen, with big fields and no time pressure. The map became the place you look, and the list became the place you write.
