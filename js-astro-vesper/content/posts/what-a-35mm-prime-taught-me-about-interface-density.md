In June I put one lens on the camera and left the others at home. By August I had stopped thinking about framing as something the lens does. I walked closer, or I walked away.

Dense screens have the same problem as a zoom lens. When everything can be resized, nothing has to earn its place. The crew view in Ridgeline started with twelve adjustable panels. It shipped with three fixed ones.

## Fixed sizes, honest choices

A fixed column width forces the question a zoom never asks: is this worth the space? Most of the time the answer was no, and the panel went to a second screen one tap away.

The result reads faster. People stop scanning for the thing they need, because there are only three places it can be.
