Ridgeline is an offline map and trail report tool for the crews and rangers who keep trails open. It is used on 1,900 miles of trail in three states.

Every screen renders from what is already on the phone, and the network only ever adds to it. Crews write reports at the end of the day; the next time anyone on the crew gets a signal, everyone's reports sync.

Switchback Studio built Ridgeline for a parks group in Washington and keeps it running.
